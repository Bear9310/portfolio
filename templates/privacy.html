Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this website (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit or use our portfolio site.

We may collect the following types of information:

This site may use Google AdSense and other third-party advertising services. Google and other ad partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

For more information about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Cookies

We use cookies for essential site functionality, analytics, and advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site and the relevance of ads you see.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the site (hosting, analytics)

6. Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained so we can respond and keep a record of inquiries. You may request deletion of your contact data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact form or email listed on this site.

8. Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please use the Contact form.
